We're here to help, every day.

Frequently asked questions

What are the park hours?

Daily from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Promenade shops close 30 minutes later.

What happens if I stay after closing?

Security Animas will escort you to Guest Relations, where you can wait until the park reopens.

Does the park run during city blackouts?

Yes. Orpheus Realms has its own solar power and grid. The park never goes dark.

Can Animas cause harm?

Never to a registered person. Every Anima operates under the Orpheus Directive.

What if an Anima doesn't recognize me?

Check that your wristband is registered. If the problem persists, visit Guest Relations.

I lost something in the park.

Lost & Found is next to Guest Relations. Everything our Sweepers find is kept there, with no expiration date.

Is there medical care?

There are first-aid stations in every realm, marked with a cross on the map.

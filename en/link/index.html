Orpheus Link

Your wristband. Your story. Forever.

  1. Register your wristbandAt the gate, every guest receives an Orpheus Link. Register it with your name and preferences at the Dawn Gate.
  2. Live your adventureEvery Anima you meet will keep what you shared: your deeds, your favorites and your role in their realm.
  3. Come back anytimeNext time, your story picks up right where it left off. Your characters will be waiting.

Wristband questions

What does my wristband store?

Your name, preferences, your story in each realm, purchases and prizes. Data is kept for as long as your registration exists.

What if I lose it?

Your story isn't lost: it lives in the Orpheus Mind. Register a new wristband at Guest Relations.

Can I enter without a wristband?

No. Animas can only recognize and accompany registered guests.

For your safety, Animas can only recognize and accompany guests with a registered wristband. Unregistered persons will be directed to Guest Relations.

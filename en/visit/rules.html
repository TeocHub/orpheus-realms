Park rules

So every visit is safe and magical.

  1. Wear your wristband at all times.Without it, your characters can't recognize you or save your story.
  2. Respect park hours.The park closes at 10:00 PM. After closing, our Security Animas will kindly escort anyone remaining on the grounds to Guest Relations.
  3. Guests without a registered wristband.Animas cannot identify unregistered persons. They will be directed to Guest Relations to complete registration.
  4. Use emergency exits only in an emergency.When opened, they trigger an alarm across the sector and alert our Security team.
  5. Service doors are for cast members only.Thank you for keeping the magic: behind every realm are hallways that aren't part of the story.
  6. No tools or sharp objects.For the safety of our Animas and guests, all bags are checked at the Dawn Gate.
  7. Do not disassemble or move Animas.If an Anima doesn't respond, tell any Usher. Our maintenance units will take care of it.
  8. Lost something?Every item found is taken to Lost & Found, next to Guest Relations. Items are kept indefinitely.

The Animas

Characters who believe in their story, and in you.

Every Anima in the park runs on the Orpheus Mind. It truly lives its role: it knows who you are, remembers what you shared and adapts its adventure to what you like. That's why no two visits are ever the same.

Realm hosts

Sir BrezoKingdom of Altamira

Knight of the Order of Dawn. He searches for lost squires and brings them safely to the castle.

Captain MareaLantern Sea

Recruits a crew for the great voyage. Remembers every guest's post aboard her ship.

The Great BastiánGrand Star Fair

Ringmaster. Nobody has ever beaten him at his favorite game.

Gardener AlbaGardens of Orpheus

Tends every plant in the park, including the seed you left on your first visit.

LumenShopping Promenade

Remembers your size, your colors and what you looked at last time.

The HostessThe whole park

The voice of the Realms. She greets you by name and never forgets a visit.

The cast you don't see

Behind every realm, a team of service Animas keeps everything running.

UshersSecurity and wayfinding

They help you find your way and escort anyone who needs it to Guest Relations. They respond to any alarm within seconds.

SweepersCleaning

They keep the park spotless day and night. If you leave something on the ground, they'll pick it up for you.

Animas can only recognize guests with a registered Orpheus Link wristband. Every Anima operates under the Orpheus Directive.

Grand Star Fair

Lights that never stop spinning, organ music and the most famous challenge in the park.

The Great Bastián is the ringmaster of the Star Fair. He knows every guest by name and keeps count of every prize won.

His favorite game is as simple as it is impossible: when the Great Bastián looks at you, you cannot move. Whoever moves, loses.

Things to do

  • ShowThe Star Spectacle

    Acrobats, lights and the Great Bastián in the Big Top. Daily at 9:00 PM.

  • RideThe Constellation Wheel

    A 44-meter wheel overlooking all six realms. Closed, climate-controlled cabins.

  • Live gameWhoever Moves, Loses

    The Great Bastián's challenge. Winners receive a prize loaded onto their wristband.

Gardens of Orpheus

The quietest realm in the park: flowers of all four seasons, glass greenhouses and a seed with your name on it.

Gardener Alba tends every plant in the Gardens of Orpheus. On your first visit she gives you a seed; you plant it in the Greenhouse and come back to watch it grow.

Our gardening units work all night so the gardens are perfect every morning, rain or shine, whatever happens.

Things to do

  • MazeThe Maze of Seasons

    Four gardens, one for each season, joined by a hedge maze.

  • ExperienceSeed of Your Own Greenhouse

    Plant your seed and register it to your wristband. Gardener Alba will tell you how it grew.

  • WalkThe Gardeners' Path

    A guided walk through the gardens that supply the park's kitchens.

Gardener AlbaKeeper of the Gardens

“This seed is yours. I will look after it until you come back, however long it takes.”

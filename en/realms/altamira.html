Kingdom of Altamira

A kingdom of towers, banners and tournaments where every child can become a squire, and every squire a knight.

Long ago, the Order of Dawn protected the kingdom of Altamira. But one night its squires were lost in the torchlit forest, and Sir Brezo has searched for them ever since.

Your first visit begins your training. Sir Brezo will recognize you every time you return, remember your deeds and give you new quests inside the kingdom.

Things to do

  • ShowThe Torch Tournament

    Jousts, banners and controlled fire. Daily at 12:30 PM and 7:30 PM in the Dawn Arena.

  • Dark rideThe Squire's Tower

    Climb the tower in a wooden cart and face the trials of the Order. Minimum height: 100 cm.

  • WorkshopThe Dawn Armory

    Design a shield with your family's colors. It is saved to your wristband.

Sir BrezoKnight of Dawn

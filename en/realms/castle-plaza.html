Castle Plaza

The castle you can see from anywhere in the park, the plaza where parades begin and the voice that welcomes you.

Castle Plaza connects all six realms. The Dawn Parade sets out from here every afternoon, and the castle lights up here every night to bid our guests goodbye.

The Hostess speaks to you through speakers across the park. She greets you by name as you enter, announces the shows and reminds you of closing time.

Things to do

  • ParadeThe Dawn Parade

    Every Anima in the park marches down Entrance Street to the Plaza. Daily at 6:00 PM.

  • ShowThe Castle Illumination

    The castle lights up in the colors of all six realms. Daily at 9:45 PM.

  • ServicesGuest Relations and Lost & Found

    Wristband registration, assistance and found items, next to the Dawn Gate.

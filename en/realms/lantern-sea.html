Lantern Sea

Calm canals, floating lanterns and a ship forever about to set sail on the great voyage.

Captain Marea is preparing the greatest voyage of her life, but she needs a crew. Every guest who climbs aboard gets a post: deckhand, lookout, cartographer.

At dusk the Lantern Sea canals light up with hundreds of lanterns. The more you sail with the Captain, the higher you rise in her crew.

Things to do

  • Boat rideLantern Boats

    Ride the canals in a six-person boat. The lantern parade begins at 5:00 PM.

  • Dark rideThe Captain's Hold

    A boat journey through the ship's holds, among maps, treasure and storms.

  • LookoutThe Wishing Lighthouse

    Write a wish on a paper lantern. The Captain will keep it for your next visit.

Captain MareaCaptain of the Lantern Sea

Shopping Promenade

The happiest street in the park: shops, workshops and the official photo with your Anima.

On the Shopping Promenade you'll find everything your favorite characters wear in their realm: Order of Dawn capes, the Captain's hats, the Great Bastián's noses.

Lumen, the friendliest clerk in the park, remembers your size, your favorite colors and what you looked at on your last visit.

Things to do

  • ShopCostume Workshop

    Costumes from every realm, tailored to fit while you wait.

  • ShopThe Realm Plush Shop

    Plushies of every Anima, each with a message recorded by its character.

  • ExperiencePhoto with Your Anima

    Your official photo with the character of your choice, saved to your wristband.
